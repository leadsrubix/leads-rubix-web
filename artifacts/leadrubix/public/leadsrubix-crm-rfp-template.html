The 10 questions

  1. Lead capture latency: What's the median time from a Facebook / Instagram lead-form submit to the lead landing in our agent's screen with a notification? Show us logs from a live customer.
  2. Round-robin and re-routing: How does your rotation handle agent leave, off-hours, and SLA breach? Can rules be different per source / project / city?
  3. Call logging: Are calls auto-logged with GPS, duration, recording, and disposition? On Android and iOS? What's your fallback when an agent denies microphone permission?
  4. WhatsApp Business API: Are you a Meta BSP or do we need a separate vendor? How are template approvals handled and what conversation pricing applies in INR?
  5. RERA / DPDP / GST: How do you support RERA disclosures, DPDP consent capture and erasure SLAs, GST-compliant invoicing with our GSTIN and IRN?
  6. Data residency & backups: Where is our data stored? Is it in an Indian region? Backup frequency, restore SLA, and how we exfiltrate the full dataset on demand.
  7. Roles & access: What permission levels exist? Can a manager mask phone numbers from junior agents? Audit log retention?
  8. Reporting: Out-of-the-box dashboards we'd see on day one — by source, agent, project, stage, and channel. Can we export raw data via API?
  9. Integrations: Native (not Zapier) integrations with: 99acres / Housing / MagicBricks / IndiaMART / Justdial / Razorpay / Tally / Zoho Books / Google Workspace.
  10. Pricing & lock-in: All-in monthly INR cost for our team size including WhatsApp BSP, support tier, and any per-seat overage. Contract length, exit clause, and notice period.
